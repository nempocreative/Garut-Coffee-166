description: Keeps product packaging isolated inside gift-box previews without changing catalog imagery.

Use transparent, product-only cutouts for items shown inside the open gift box. Keep the original lifestyle images for catalog and product-selection cards, and preserve the package artwork rather than recreating it. Every selectable product needs a matching cutout.

**Why:** the product catalog photos include scenic backgrounds, while the open-box preview should show the actual packaging only.

**How to apply:** when adding a gift-builder product, add its cutout asset using the same product ID. Verify the open-box view has no photo backdrop and custom text remains limited to the closed-box view.

Do not repeat the package contents as a thumbnail/name strip beneath the box preview.

**Why:** the user asked to remove the lower “ISI PAKET” gallery and its explanatory note.

**How to apply:** keep product choices in the selector controls and show the selected items inside the open-box preview only.
